# Licensing proposal — owner review required

Agentic UI is proposed as **source-available software with personal noncommercial
permission**, not OSI-approved open source. The [draft LICENSE](../LICENSE) is the
controlling text and takes precedence over this explanation. It is not yet approved
and grants no new permission until Elias Etl approves it for release. A passing
build or CI run does not approve legal wording. This PR must remain unmerged until
the owner reviews the license.

Elias Etl has confirmed ownership of the first-party code and is its author and
proposed licensor. Third-party material keeps its own licenses; see
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Confirmed policy and examples

The intended permission is free use by individuals for their own personal,
noncommercial purposes. After approval, examples include an unpaid personal
learning project or a hobby dashboard that is neither monetized nor operated
for an employer, client, business, or organization.

Prior written authorization is required for a paid client website, an internal
business dashboard, a commercial app, or a service that incorporates or relies
on the library. Working as an individual does not exempt paid or business work.
There is no automatic organization/nonprofit or revenue-based exemption.

For initial contact, request contact with Elias Etl through the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/etleli/agentic-ui/issues). Do not post confidential
business information. Filing an issue does not itself authorize use. Pricing,
support, and commercial rights belong in a separate written agreement.

The commercial restriction is why this proposal does not meet the unrestricted
field-of-use requirements of the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd).

## Drafting choices that need approval

- **Modification and sharing:** allow personal modification and no-charge sharing
  of original or modified library code under the same restrictions. Keep the
  license and notices, identify modifications, and avoid suggesting endorsement.
  This is proposed detail, not an already-confirmed owner decision.
- **Termination:** automatic termination for material breach, with automatic
  reinstatement for a first breach fully cured within 30 days of discovery or
  notice; otherwise written reinstatement is required. Earlier liability remains.
- **Warranty and liability:** no warranty or support commitment and broad liability
  exclusions, limited by non-waivable law. These provisions need legal review for
  the intended distribution and jurisdictions; technical checks do not establish
  their enforceability.
- **Scope:** no claim over users' original applications, data, layouts, or changes;
  no source disclosure for unrelated application code; no UI branding requirement.
  Third-party rights remain separate. Approved copies keep their granted terms
  even if future releases use a revised license.

No fee, subscription, mandatory support purchase, contact email, governing-law,
arbitration, or venue term is introduced. Before release, approve or revise the
actual LICENSE and remove its draft marker in a reviewed commit. Follow the
[publication procedure](publishing.md); publication is still blocked.
